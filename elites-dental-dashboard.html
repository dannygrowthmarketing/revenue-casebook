03

Turn directions and clicks into calls and bookings

Nine moves, ranked. Each names the number it should change.

Ads · todayTop up the prepaid balance

The rebuilt campaign is eligible but not serving. Every day off loses about 42 clicks at today's rate.

KPI: clicks per day back to 40+
Tracking · week 1Count calls from ads and the site

Google forwarding number on the call asset, calls over 60 seconds as the main conversion, and a website call-click event.

KPI: tracked conversions per ₹1,000
Tracking · week 1Send the booking form and WhatsApp back to Ads

Only 1 of 680 clicks was counted. Without this, Maximise conversions has nothing to learn from.

KPI: cost per tracked lead
Calls · profileMake the call the obvious next step

Calls fell to 30 in September. Add "Call for a same-day slot" posts, the call button in every post, and hours that show Sunday clearly.

KPI: calls per 100 views, from 2.2
Calls · clinicCheck who answers

Ring the profile number at 10 am, 2 pm and 7 pm on a weekday and a Sunday. A missed first call is a lost patient.

KPI: answered within 3 rings
Directions · profileHelp the 711 who asked for directions

Photos of the CMS Towers entrance and first-floor sign, a landmark in the description, and parking in the attributes.

KPI: share of directions that become visits
Directions · adsShow the address in every ad

Link the profile as a location asset so ads carry distance and a directions button for "near me" searches.

KPI: directions from ads
Bookings · profileAdd a booking link Google accepts

0 bookings in six months. Point the profile's appointment link at the site's booking page, with UTM tags.

KPI: bookings from the profile, from 0
Ads · structureShift money to problem searches

Root canal, smile correction and kids' dentistry click 4–9× better than "dental clinic near me". Give them their own ad groups and pages.
