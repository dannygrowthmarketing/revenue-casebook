Elites Dental Care

A clinic rated 4.9 by 271 patients paid ₹3,140 for each booking. The ads were clicked. So where did the patients go?

The case in eight lines

Context
Family practice since 2006, five MDS specialists. Routine work is frequent and cheap; implants and aligners are rare and worth 20–50× more per case.
Problem
One Search campaign, 4 Aug–17 Sep 2026: ₹25,122, 469 clicks, 8 bookings. Paused. The clinic read it as "Google doesn't work for us".
Evidence
Google Ads exports (campaign, schedule, keywords, 1,029 search terms, device, demographics, reach), GA4, Search Console, Business Profile performance, live site measurements, Maps and SERP checks.
Diagnosis
Nothing before the click was broken. Everything after it was: slow mobile page, a 4-step booking widget, every booking valued at ₹1, and no presence where patients compare clinics.
Action
Business Profile rebuilt live on 27 Sep. Search campaign rebuilt: 7 ad groups, 76 keywords, budget follows the searches that booked. Fix list for site, tracking and directories.
Outcome
Relaunch planned for 1 Oct 2026. Results not yet available.
Limitations
No attendance or treatment revenue shared. Five of eight bookings landed on one day and are unverified against the appointment book.
Next step
Get the booking widget working, pass real treatment values, claim directories, publish cost pages.

03

How I found it

Split the spend by what patients searched, by hour, and by what happened after the click.

Where ₹25,122 went, by search theme

Implants, braces and aligners, worth ₹35,000–80,000 a case, got ₹908. A catch-all ad group took 67% of impressions; the Implants group got 75 impressions in seven weeks. Measured

Spend by ad-schedule slot

Teal slots booked. Sunday mornings: 42 clicks, 5 bookings, ₹421 each. Mornings converted 2.89%, evenings 1.01%, yet 62% of budget ran in evenings. Measured

Reviews: rating isn't the differentiator

* Advertises into the area from outside it. Every clinic on the road is rated 4.5+. Prodent (253 reviews) and DentStop (125) outrank Elites on page one with fewer reviews, because they built pages and listings for the term. Measured, Google Maps 18 Sep

Business Profile actions, Apr–Sep 2026

9,572 profile views, 1,031 actions. 70% asked for directions; bookings from the profile: zero. Measured

Most ad clicks never reached analytics

469 ad clicks in Aug–Sep became 73 paid sessions in GA4: about 1 in 6. Mobile load measured 5.3 s in a later check. GA4 had no key events and wasn't linked to Ads.

Calculated from GA4 and Ads, read 30 Sep

251 price searches with nowhere to land

Searches like "root canal treatment cost in bangalore" reached the ads 494 times and converted nothing. Nine of ten page-one results publish a price. Elites publishes none.

The site signs itself with a competitor's name

Service page titles end "| Elite Dental", the name of a different clinic 1.2 km away. No H1, no meta description, no Dentist schema. 41 junk URLs indexed, including 13 Latin placeholder posts from 2015.

04

Diagnosis

The account was short of direction, not budget. The cheapest fixes need no media at all.

Post-click friction

2,610 ms server response on mobile, where 96% of spend landed. A booking flow that asks you to choose between one clinic. On 27 Sep the widget loaded blank: the vendor's SSL certificate had expired, which also broke the conversion tag listening to it.

Bidding can't tell an implant from a scaling

Every booking passed a value of ₹1. No offline import of who attended or paid.

Money on the cheapest intent

"dental clinic near me" was the largest line: ₹6,529, 124 clicks, 0 bookings. Seven of eight bookings came from three search terms and ₹3,237.

Invisible where patients compare

Not on Practo, Lybrate or Bajaj Health. Justdial filed under a third locality name. Zero page-one presence for "dental clinic tc palya". Four different spellings of the locality across the web.

05

What I changed

Order matters: fix the page and tracking before restarting spend.

Business Profile, rebuilt live · 27 Sep

300 service entries (275 stuffed with "near me" and the wrong locality) cut to 121 across 54 real services, 50 rewritten in plain language. New 681-character description with the right locality and specialists. Wrong service areas removed. Booking link moved to https with a UTM tag.

Done on the live profile

Search campaign, rebuilt · launch planned 1 Oct

Seven ad groups, 76 exact and phrase keywords, no broad match, AI Max off. Target 70% absolute-top impression share with an ₹85 CPC cap, ₹1,000 a day. Schedule tilted to mornings. 150+ negatives. No audience segments, to stay inside Google's health-ads policy.

Switch to Maximize conversions only after 15 recorded conversions

Fix list handed to the clinic

Renew the widget certificate, caching and CDN for the 2.6 s response, real booking values, GA4 key events and Ads link, title tags and schema, delete the junk URLs, claim four directories, six doctor profiles, six cost pages.

06

What happened

The baseline is measured. The relaunch result isn't in yet, and this page won't pretend otherwise.

What the same budget could do

At the measured ₹53.56 CPC, ₹50,000 buys about 933 clicks. At last flight's 1.71% that is 16 bookings. At 4%, a third of what Sunday mornings delivered, it is 37 bookings at ₹1,339 each.

Estimate The 4% rate is a target, not a result. Attendance and treatment value are assumptions the clinic must replace.

07

What's blocked and unproven

Five bookings on one Sunday

16 August: 5 bookings from 14 clicks, a 36% same-day rate. Could be one family booking several slots, or the script firing twice. If it's the second, true cost per booking is nearer ₹8,374. Needs the appointment book.

Vendor and clinic dependencies

The booking widget certificate sits with the vendor. First-visit value, doctor specialties, price ranges and Sunday-slot sign-off sit with the clinic. My GA4 role couldn't open Recent events.

Profile calls fell

Calls from the profile went from 42 in August to 18 by 27 September. Too large to be reporting lag alone. Watching weekly.

08

What I'd do next

Weeks 1–3 · no media spend

Widget certificate, server speed, real conversion values, title tags, schema, index clean-up, directories, doctor profiles.

Weeks 3–7 · restart properly

Treatment ad groups with their own pages; call-only for pain and emergency; generic "near me" capped as a test.

Weeks 6–13 · take the ground

Cost pages for implants, aligners, root canal and full-mouth rehab. A TC Palya location page. Reviews that name the treatment. Offline conversion import from practice records.

Limits. Impression share and lost IS were not in the export. Ad-group spend comes from the search-terms report, which totals ₹22,016 of ₹25,122; proportions hold, absolutes are a floor. Rank checks are single-point and signed in.
