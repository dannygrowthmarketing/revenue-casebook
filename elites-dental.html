03

What the history shows

Patterns found by re-reading the full history, not just the months I ran. Each is labelled: measured pattern, correlation, anomaly or seasonality.

Anomaly

The morning edge is one Sunday, not a pattern

Sunday morning shows 11.9% conversion and 5 of 8 bookings. All five fall on 16 Aug. The other three Sundays spent ₹3,000 for nothing. Mon-Sat mornings took 29% of spend and booked nothing. Weekend 4.0% against weekday 0.63% shrinks to 0.74% against 0.63% without 16 Aug.

The numbers

Daily spend reconciles to slots by weekday (Sunday ₹3,668 = Sun AM ₹2,107 + Sun PM ₹1,561; four Sundays delivered). 16 Aug: ₹668, 14 clicks, 5 bookings. Other Sundays ₹532/1,075/1,393, 0 bookings. Mon-Sat mornings: 131 clicks, ₹7,347, 0 bookings (weekday mornings ₹58 CPC). Evenings 296 clicks, ₹15,668, 3 bookings (15 Aug Sat, 8 Sep Tue, 9 Sep Wed). Weekday 2 of 319 clicks (0.63%); weekend 6 of 150 (4.0%); weekend ex-16 Aug 1 of 136 (0.74%).

So whatMorning-weighting in the relaunch rests on n=1. Keep it as a labelled test with a cap. Check the 16 Aug rows against the appointment book before reading day-part.

Anomaly

'89% of spend on no-booking days' is what chance looks like

With 8 bookings across 35 spend days, even scattering gives about 80% no-booking days. The signal is clustering and spikes: 5 bookings in one day, and the 8 days over ₹1,000 (41% of spend) booked nothing. Two terms show more conversions than clicks allow, hinting at double counting.

The numbers

Poisson at 0.23 bookings a day: 80% of days empty; chance of 5 or more in one day about 4 in a million. Observed 31 of 35 spend days empty (89%). Days over ₹1,000: 13 Aug, 17 Aug, 23 Aug, 30 Aug, 31 Aug, 5 Sep, 16 Sep, 17 Sep = ₹10,197, 0 bookings. Booking days spent ₹574-861. Daily spend ranged ₹112-1,475 (CV 49%). 'kids dentist bangalore': 1 click, 2 conversions. 'dentist near me near me': 6 clicks, 2 conversions.

So whatDo not use the 89% as a diagnosis. Test the conversion tag for double-firing and for several conversions per click before trusting ₹3,140. Add a daily budget cap to stop spike days.

Anomaly

Term-level conversions contradict each other, and the planned negatives would cut bookings

The spend table gives 'dentist near me' 0 conversions and calls it the biggest drain; the booking table credits it 3 at ₹960. 'Dentist near me near me' shows 0 and 2. Five of 8 bookings sit under terms marked 0. 'near me near me' is on the negative list. All 7 attributed bookings came from the catch-all group; the six treatment groups are untested, not failed.

The numbers

'dentist near me': 68 clicks, ₹3,855, 0 conv (spend table) vs 55 clicks, ₹2,879, 3 conv (booking table). 'dentist near me near me': 6 clicks, ₹308, 0 vs 2 conv. Negative 'near me near me' would block terms behind 2 of 8 bookings. Original group: 7 conv on ₹16,317 (₹2,331 each). Six treatment groups: 15 clicks, ₹798; 1.71% expects 0.26 bookings. 3 terms gave 7 bookings on 62 clicks (11.3%). Break-even at 60% attendance (assumed): ₹3,140 / 0.6 = ₹5,233 per attended patient.

So whatRe-pull search terms with conversions by conversion time before adding negatives. Do not cut 'dentist near me' phrases. Give treatment groups volume before judging them. Break-even needs clinic values and attendance.

Correlation, not causation

About 5 in 6 paid clicks may never become a counted visit

GA4 saw roughly 1 in 6 ad clicks. The page takes 2.6 s to respond and 3.3 s to paint on mobile, and 96% of spend is mobile. Ads clicks are not visits, so 1.71% on clicks understates conversion among those who load the page and the loss sits before the widget.

The numbers

469 clicks; ~78 GA4 sessions (469/6, second-hand); 8/78 = about 10% of counted sessions. Spend on unseen clicks about ₹20,900 (5/6 of ₹25,122). Server response 2,610 ms, first paint 3,336 ms, 2.6 MB, 162 requests; ₹24,090 of spend on mobile (448 clicks); no tag manager. Report calls the 469 'qualified visits'.

So whatSpeed and tag firing come before ad rebuild. After relaunch, compare Ads clicks with GA4 sessions by day with UTM tags to size the loss. GA4 export is not in sources, so the 1-in-6 needs checking.

Correlation, not causation

Profile calls fell about 51% per day, ahead of any change, and 'near me' runs through three channels

Per day, September calls (to 27 Sep) run at half the Apr-Aug rate. Ads starting on 4 Aug did not lift calls (Aug 42 vs Jul 45), and the data ends on the day of the rebuild. 'Near me' sits in 76% of ad spend, 275 of 300 profile services and the Google Posts, while the report's signed-out read saw no services at all.

The numbers

Calls per day: Apr 1.17, May 1.29, Jun 1.50, Jul 1.45, Aug 1.35, Sep 0.67 (18 in 27 days). Apr-Aug average 1.35; Sep -51%. Projected full September about 20 calls. Lost about 22 calls a month vs ads delivering about 5.3 bookings a month (8 in 45 days). 300 services, 275 'near me'; 'Services listed: None' in the 18 Sep report.

So whatWeekly profile data would show whether the drop began before 18 Sep. The stuffed services list is a candidate cause, not proven. Profile calls outnumber paid bookings about 8 to 1, so protect them first.

Not a marketing problem

What the data shows ads cannot fix. Saying so early saves budget.

  • Booking widget: 4 steps and an expired SSL certificate sit with the vendor (click4appointment).
  • Server response 2,610 ms with no cache or CDN, and staging and payment-named test pages publicly reachable and in the sitemap: developer and host.
  • Treatment values, attendance and which cases are profitable: clinic data not shared.
  • Price publication (₹16k-95k implant range; nine of ten page-one rivals publish prices): clinic decision.
  • Brand collision: elitedentalcare.co.in is a different clinic and ranks first for the brand name. Titles saying 'Elite Dental' were the clinic's own error.
  • Review volume trails SmileFit (798), KKS (614), Clove (398): needs a patient review routine.

Data not available

What would prove more, and does not exist in the sources yet.

  • Appointment-book check of the 16 Aug and other booking days.
  • GA4 and Search Console exports: the 1-in-6 figure is my reading; the report had no GA4 access.
  • Business Profile calls by week or day, and the original export (only monthly values, September to the 27th); directions and website clicks.
  • Treatment values, attendance rate, repeat visits, revenue per patient.
  • Impression share and lost share, ad creatives, bid strategy; click counts per day (only 16 Aug is given).
  • Why delivery stopped 10-15 Sep and whether the widget certificate expiry date matches.
  • Search-term conversions by conversion time (report tables conflict).
  • Any result after 1 Oct relaunch.
  • Report keyword themes sum to ₹25,024 against ₹25,122; ad-group table is a floor (₹22,016).
