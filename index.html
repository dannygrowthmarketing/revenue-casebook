How I work

The same five moves on every engagement. Each one has a real example behind it.

Diagnose

Trace the journey from search to till. Check the measurement before trusting any result.

MR Jewels: Google reported ₹12.56 Cr of value in a ₹1.07 Cr month.

Strategize

Rank fixes by rupees recovered per hour. Separate marketing problems from ops problems.

TONI&GUY: the biggest number was average bill, a shop-floor lever worth ₹17.78 L a month.

Implement

Build it myself: campaigns, feeds, profiles, pages, tracking, apps.

25 Google and 26 Meta campaigns live across 15 salons.

Measure

Grade every figure: measured, calculated, estimated or unavailable. Show both sources when they disagree.

Three conflicting cost totals shown side by side, not silently picked.

Optimize

Set stop rules before spend scales. Write down what failed.

Demand Gen at ₹790 per lead vs ₹33.71 average: switched off.

How to read the numbers

Every figure on this site carries one of four labels. Nothing estimated is shown as measured.

Measured

Read straight from a platform export, point-of-sale sheet, Shopify or Business Profile.

Calculated

Derived from measured figures. The formula is shown next to it.

Estimate

Modelled or assumed. Always says what it assumed.

Data unavailable

The business didn't have it, or didn't share it. Shown as a gap, never filled in.
