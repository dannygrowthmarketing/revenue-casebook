02

What I solve

Eight places revenue leaks.

Most businesses don't have a traffic problem. They have one of these. Each links to the cases where I found it.

  1. 01Miscounted conversionsThe ad platform reports sales the till never saw. Bidding chases fiction.5/7 cases

    The signalPlatform conversions above real orders. Value per conversion far from average order.

    The fixRead conversion actions, not goal cards. Reconcile to the till weekly. Stop rule at 60%.

    Seen inTONI&GUY, MR Jewels, Elites Dental, Gold Coins Resort, EveryWatch

  2. 02Money on the wrong intentBudget sits where it is easy to spend, not where buyers convert.5/7 cases

    The signalShare of spend and share of results don't match. One ad group starves the rest.

    The fixRank spend by revenue per rupee at the unit that bills. Cap, kill or fund.

    Seen inTONI&GUY, MR Jewels, Elites Dental, Gold Coins Resort, Foremost PA

  3. 03Post-click frictionThe ad works. The page, form or checkout after it doesn't.5/7 cases

    The signalHealthy CTR, poor conversion. Results move while spend and CPC stay flat.

    The fixBook it yourself on a phone. Cut steps, fix speed, price and phone above the fold.

    Seen inMR Jewels, Elites Dental, Gold Coins Resort, Foremost PA, Swift Restoration

  4. 04Invisible where buyers lookMissing from the map pack, directories, page one, even brand search.4/7 cases

    The signalCompetitors with fewer reviews rank above you. Your own name shows someone else.

    The fixProfile, listings, service and location pages. Defend the brand term.

    Seen inElites Dental, Gold Coins Resort, Foremost PA, Swift Restoration

  5. 05Price and channel leakageYour own stock sold cheaper elsewhere, with commission on top.1/7 cases

    The signalGoogle Hotels or marketplaces list third parties below your direct price.

    The fixOne rate card, one owner, weekly parity check, best-rate-direct promise.

    Seen inGold Coins Resort

  6. 06Lead-to-booking gapLeads arrive at record volume. Nobody converts them fast enough.2/7 cases

    The signalLeads up, bookings flat or down. Calls fall while direction requests rise.

    The fixEvery lead to the right person's WhatsApp in five minutes. Mystery-shop peak hours.

    Seen inTONI&GUY, Gold Coins Resort

  7. 07Post-sale leakageThe sale happens. The money doesn't stay or doesn't come back.3/7 cases

    The signalHigh returns, falling ticket size, one-time buyers.

    The fixReturns report by SKU and checkout. Ticket-size work on the floor. Lifecycle triggers.

    Seen inTONI&GUY, MR Jewels, EveryWatch

  8. 08Not a marketing problemSometimes the biggest number isn't mine to fix. Saying so early saves months.3/7 cases

    The signalThe same complaint in reviews. A gap no ad change moves.

    The fixName the owner: ops, product, pricing or the shop floor. Put a rupee value on it.

    Seen inTONI&GUY, MR Jewels, Gold Coins Resort

07

Business results

What seven businesses have in common.

Patterns that came out of the evidence, not a framework I started with.

P1

Volume up, value per lead down

Three businesses bought more traffic and got less per visitor. More leads is not the goal; more bills, bookings or orders is.

  • TONI&GUY: revenue per Google lead ₹3,116 → ₹1,361
  • Gold Coins Resort: enquiries +46%, bookings −13%, Q2 → Q3
  • MR Jewels: Aug sessions +53.3%, store conversion −24.2%
P2

The platform number is not the business number

Ad platforms counted things the till never saw. Every case starts by reconciling to orders, bills or the CRM.

  • MR Jewels: Google claimed 11.8× the company's revenue
  • Elites Dental: every booking valued at ₹1
  • TONI&GUY: 6–12 Aug conversion rate 19.3% vs 1.7% other days
  • EveryWatch: a reported return was lead counts × a fixed value
P3

Every revenue chain breaks somewhere

In 7 of 7 cases the evidence from ad rupee to revenue stops at one link. I show exactly where, instead of filling the gap with a ROAS figure.

  • TONI&GUY: breaks at “Lead to booked chair” (link 4 of 6)
  • MR Jewels: breaks at “Profit” (link 7 of 7)
  • Elites Dental: breaks at “Patients attended, treatment value, revenue” (link 6 of 6)
  • Gold Coins Resort: breaks at “Enquiries tagged to an ad” (link 3 of 7)
  • Foremost PA: breaks at “Signed claims” (link 4 of 6)
  • EveryWatch: breaks at “Subscriptions or dealer revenue” (link 5 of 6)
  • Swift Restoration: breaks at “Google Search and LSA” (link 1 of 6)
P4

  6. Loss aversion
