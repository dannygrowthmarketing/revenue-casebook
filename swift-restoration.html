II.2

What the history shows

Patterns found by re-reading the full history, not just the months I ran. Each is labelled: measured pattern, correlation, anomaly or seasonality.

Anomaly

The noindex percentage and the crawl size do not match

Sources say 42 noindex URLs = 24.85% of a crawl of ~93 URLs. 42 / 93 is 45.2%. 24.85% only works on a base of ~169 URLs. One of the two bases is wrong, or the crawl included non-HTML files, so I quote the count, 42, not a share.

The numbers

42 / 0.2485 = 169. 42 / 93 = 45.2%. ~85 attachment URLs / ~93 = 91% of the crawl. ~7 real pages.

So whatState one base. On the ~93 base, nearly half of crawled URLs are noindex and about 9 in 10 are junk attachments.

Anomaly

'Duplicate' titles are 'Multiple' in the crawl export

The case study says duplicate titles, metas and H1s across ~48-51 URLs. The competitor PDF names the export filters as 'Page Titles: Multiple = 51' and 'H1: Multiple = 47', flagged as plugin or module conflicts. 'Multiple' means more than one tag on a page, a different fault with a different fix.

The numbers

Titles multiple 51 URLs. H1 multiple 47 URLs. README range 48-51. Overlap with the ~85 attachment URLs: DATA NOT AVAILABLE.

So whatConfirm the filter before the migration spec. If the 51 are mostly attachment pages, the real-page metadata problem is smaller than stated.

Anomaly

The review target is far above the review history

15 reviews since 2018 is about 0.16 a month if all came since founding. The plan asks for 25 reviews in 30 days (+10) and 45+ in 90 days (+30). That is 24x to 64x the historical pace, depending on when reviews started.

The numbers

15 / 96 months = 0.16 a month. +10 in 30 days = 64x. If reviews began 36 months ago: 15 / 36 = 0.42 a month; 24x. Job volume per month: DATA NOT AVAILABLE.

So whatThe review engine depends on completed jobs per month, which no source gives. Treat 25 and 45 as stretch targets until job volume is known.

Anomaly

Month-1 plan conflicts with itself on sequencing

03-Current-State says do not patch WordPress, carry fixes into the .NET build. The keyword workbook roadmap says fix the Gutenverse plugin first, 'Critical, do first', and that every page build is blocked until then. Week 3 ships Water, Emergency, Boone and Ames pages. The migration date is not given. By day 60 the roadmap adds ~10 pages (5 pillars, 4 towns, 1 hub) on top of ~10, ~20 in all, against a 90-day target of ~17+ indexed pages.

The numbers

Day-30 target ~10 indexed pages. Day-60 additions: Mold, Fire, Storm, Commercial, Sewage + Ogden, Madrid, Nevada, Story City + insurance hub = 10. 90-day target ~17+. Sitemap lists 37 URLs.

So whatDecide the platform for Week 3 and fix the KPI targets. Without a migration date, none of the Month-1 page dates can be promised.

Measured pattern

Demand is sized in bands, and almost all bands are Low

Of 56 keywords across service gaps and local plan, 38 (68%) are Low band, 17 Med, 1 High. No service keyword is High. The one High band keyword is Des Moines, deferred to a later phase. 3 of the 5 'Critical' local keywords are Low.

The numbers

Service gaps: 25 Low, 10 Med, 0 High (35). Local: 13 Low, 7 Med, 1 High (21). Critical local: 3 Low, 2 Med. Bands are estimates from population and intent, not search volume.

So whatHome turf is winnable but small. No source sizes the calls available. Pull exact volume before promising lead numbers.

II.3

What ads can't fix, and what the data can't prove

Not a marketing problem

What the data shows ads cannot fix. Saying so early saves budget.

  • Possible duplicate or impersonating domain swiftrestoration-us.com (same address, different phone, 'founded 2021'). Ownership unresolved. If it is an aggregator, it could divert calls. No data on calls lost.
  • Platform migration WordPress to .NET: timing and redirect risk sit with the client's developer. Migration date not stated.
  • Who controls the Google Ads account is unclear: advertiser shown as the owner's personal name, 'Verified'. Account access was never provided.
  • Close rate, job value and insurance billing mix: no data. Not a marketing lever until measured.

Data not available

What would prove more, and does not exist in the sources yet.

  • Leads, calls, forms, bookings, jobs and revenue. No tracking was installed.
  • Google Ads account data: spend, clicks, conversions. Only two ads seen via the Transparency Center.
  • GA4 and Search Console data. Organic sessions and impressions not baselined.
  • GBP views, calls and direction requests. Review count date ('last observed') and review velocity.
  • Exact search volume and difficulty. Only High/Med/Low bands from population and intent.
  • Referring domains and competitor traffic (Site Explorer blocked).
  • Migration launch date, and what is live now on either platform.
  • My exact start date and any change confirmed live after the June 2026 audit.
  • Crawl base: whether the denominator is ~93 or ~169 URLs.
