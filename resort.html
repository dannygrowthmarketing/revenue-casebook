II.2

What the history shows

Patterns found by re-reading the full history, not just the months I ran. Each is labelled: measured pattern, correlation, anomaly or seasonality.

Anomaly

January: 1,033 enquiries on ₹15k of ads

Jan was the biggest enquiry month of the year on the smallest ad spend of the first half. Q1 brought 84% of Q3's enquiries on 9.5% of Q3's spend. Each extra enquiry has cost more as spend rose, and Meta lead cost climbed from June to September.

The numbers

Jan 1,033 enq (18.5% of 5,588), spend ₹14,959. Q1 1,862 enq / ₹52,766 (₹28 each); Q2 1,512 / ₹1,72,419 (₹114); Q3 2,214 / ₹5,53,134 (₹250). Extra spend per extra enquiry: Q2 to Q3 ₹542; Q1 to Q3 ₹1,421. Correlation of spend with enquiries: r 0.22 (Jan-Sep), 0.61 (Mar-Sep). Meta cost per dated lead: Jun ₹131, Jul ₹635, Aug ₹311, Sep ₹1,070; Aug to Sep Meta spend +81% (₹85,733), leads 340 to 179. Aug to Sep total spend +98%, enquiries -5% (720 to 682), GA4 users 3,872 to 20,962.

So whatDemand was already there before the ads scaled. Find what January was (season, group bookings or a CRM backfill; no 2025 data to test) before reading Q3 as growth. Judge spend on cost per booking once source is tagged. Meta spend is booked at campaign end month, so monthly CPL is rough.

Correlation, not causation

Conversion fell in June, before the spend jump, then flat-lined

Conversion dropped from 32% in May to 20% in June on ₹20.9k of spend, a month before Google Ads began. Jul-Sep sat at 14-15% while spend ranged ₹1.15L-2.28L. Bookings stayed 90-157 a month while enquiries ranged 272-812, so conversion follows volume, not spend. The 14% headline also counts Jan-Feb enquiries that have no status.

The numbers

Conversion Mar 33.1, Apr 24.3, May 32.4, Jun 20.3, Jul 14.9, Aug 14.4, Sep 14.1%. Bookings 90/157/94/117/121/104/96. r(conversion, enquiries) -0.94; r(conversion, spend) -0.68 (n=7). Each extra 100 enquiries added about 5 bookings (slope 0.054). 788/5,621 = 14.0%; Mar-Sep 779/3,998 = 19.5%. June also lost enquiry type on 565 of 575 rows and Meta lead sheet starts that month.

So whatExtra leads were absorbed without extra bookings: look at response speed, routing and capacity before adding spend. Report conversion on tracked months only. Check whether June's CRM or lead-sheet change altered what counts as an enquiry.

Correlation, not causation

Calls fell at every spend level; ads did not lift Maps

Calls per direction request fell from 28% to 8%. Directions stayed flat while Google spend moved from ₹9k to ₹37k, and calls fell 23% in September when Google clicks rose 10x. The call decline began in June, before Google Ads. Google's own store report credits 18% of Jul-Sep directions to ads. The profile sent 4,785 website clicks but 2 booking-link clicks.

The numbers

Calls/directions Apr 27.9%, May 23.8%, Jun 16.5%, Jul 13.0%, Aug 10.0%, Sep 7.7%. Google spend Jul/Aug/Sep ₹33,001/9,362/36,941; clicks 7,060/3,144/32,094; directions 7,560/7,270/7,369; calls 980/730/564. Ads store report 4,087 directions of 22,199 (18.4%), 224 store visits, Jul-Sep. Profile calls per CRM enquiry 5.6 (May) to 0.83 (Sep). Mid-months read from a chart, rounded to 10.

So whatCall loss is not a demand or ad-spend problem. Test the phone line, turn on chat routed to WhatsApp, and judge Performance Max on enquiries, not taps. A booking engine link would give the profile a booking path that is not a reseller.

Measured pattern

Revenue follows ticket size; Meta money sits away from where CRM enquiries sit

Average booking ranged ₹16.6k to ₹57.7k by month, so won value moves with ticket mix, not booking count. July was 45% of Q3 value; July to September ticket fell 20% and bookings 21%. Sales wins 32% of closed leads by count but 68% by value, because lost leads are small. Meta put 63% of spend on corporate and weddings; the CRM types those as 2.4% of enquiries.

The numbers

Avg booking Mar ₹57,710; Apr 24,514; May 16,634; Jun 21,270; Jul 50,534; Aug 35,638; Sep 40,506. Jul ₹61.1L of Q3 ₹1.37Cr. Aug to Sep: spend +98%, bookings -8%, value +5%, value per ad rupee 32.2x to 17.0x. Won 788 vs lost 1,657 (32%); won ₹2.69Cr vs lost ₹1.27Cr (68%); avg won ₹34.1k, avg lost ₹7.7k. Meta: corporate ₹2,47,530 + wedding ₹1,92,519 = 63% of ₹6,99,015; rooms + day-outs ₹33,259 (4.8%). CRM types: rooms + day-outs 3,599 (64%), corporate 54 + wedding 82 = 136 (2.4%); 884 untyped. Meta sheet leads: corporate 308, wedding 221, birthday 407 vs CRM-typed 54, 82, 154.

So whatJudge months on value and cost per booking by product. The CRM types look incomplete (June, 884 untyped), so reconcile the Meta lead sheet to the CRM before moving budget by product. Value by enquiry type is not in the sources.

Measured pattern

One executive is 57% of wins and 57% of open pipeline value

Exec. A handles 28% of leads and books 57% of wins. Win rate on closed leads runs 58% for Exec. A and 14% for Exec. D, who lost 702 of 1,362 leads (42% of all lost). Exec. H has 0 wins from 450 leads. 13 February birthday rows hold 43% of open value. Opens carry no value on 158 leads.

The numbers

Exec. A 1,567 handled (28.5%), 447 won (56.7%), ₹1.22Cr won (45%), open ₹8.13Cr (57% of ₹14.29Cr). Closed-lead win rate: GM 75%, A 58%, F 50%, E 43%, B 40%, C 23%, G 15%, D 14%, H 0%. A+B+D = 71% of leads, 85% of wins. Exec. E: 18 wins at ₹1.35L average, 158 of 200 open with ₹0 value. Open 2,609 = 46% of enquiries; 567 have no status. 13 rows ₹6.2Cr (₹47.7L each) leaves clean open pipeline near ₹8.1Cr. If D matched A's 28.5%: +272 wins, about ₹79L at D's ₹29.2k ticket (assumes equal lead quality).

So whatRouting and hygiene, not media: rebalance load, review the D and H queues, quote or close the ₹0 opens, delete typo rows. One person is a single point of failure for most bookings. Lead assignment rules are not in the sources.

II.3

What ads can't fix, and what the data can't prove

Not a marketing problem

What the data shows ads cannot fix. Saying so early saves budget.

  • Follow-up: bookings stay near 100 a month at any volume; conversion 33% (Mar) to 14% (Sep). Exec. D lost 702 of 1,362 leads; Exec. H won 0 of 450.
  • Pipeline hygiene: 13 birthday rows carry ₹6.2Cr (43% of open value); 567 enquiries have no status; 158 open leads carry ₹0.
  • Rate parity: room ₹1,780 on Yatra vs ₹3,999 direct (-55%); day-out ₹1,260 vs ₹1,499. Contracts and commissions not shared.
  • Rating 3.9 stars held down by food service delays, event waiters, approach road, price vs room size; 720 one- and two-star reviews.
  • Review risk: about 24 of the latest 100 five-star reviews come from accounts with 0-1 reviews; stop any incentivised practice. Management decision.
  • No online booking or payment; Google Hotels shows reseller prices, not the official site.
  • Business name has lowercase 'bangalore' and address line is 96 characters; change needs someone present for video verification (owner call).

Data not available

What would prove more, and does not exist in the sources yet.

  • Enquiry source by channel (82% logged 'IVR'; 3 Meta, 0 Google).
  • Lead timestamps, first-response time, call-answer or missed-call logs, assignment rules.
  • Won value and bookings by enquiry type and by executive per month; which executive holds the 13 February birthday rows.
  • Any 2025 or earlier data: nothing before Jan 2026, so January's spike cannot be tested for seasonality or CRM backfill.
  • Business Profile Jan-Mar 2026 (18 months retained but only Apr-Sep pulled); mid-month values read from a chart, rounded to 10.
  • Daily Meta spend: monthly figures sit in each campaign's end month, so monthly cost ratios swing.
  • Meta lead sheet matched to CRM rows (1,194 unique leads; 237 undated; 13 marked won).
  • Why all Google ads stopped 17 Aug-6 Sep and what Meta did in those weeks.
  • What changed in the CRM or lead sheet in June (565 of 575 untyped; Meta sheet starts).
  • OTA share of revenue, commissions, net rates, direct vs OTA bookings; invoiced revenue and margin.
  • Any result after 28 Sep: Business Profile and Oct data cover one day.

IV.1
