Reviews are the moat

Median reviews per competitor. Varthur's market is deeper: a typical rival has nearly twice the reviews of a Thanisandra rival.

What the audit found

  • Distance decides. 20 rivals sit within 3 km of Thanisandra and 13 within 3 km of Varthur, where the biggest names (Ethnix, Green Trends, Profile) are 3.5–4.2 km west.
  • Ratings don't separate anyone. The median rival is 4.7★ at both sites, so the listing has to win on photos, prices and proof.
  • The brand is already on the map. Other TONI&GUY and Essensuals salons nearby will collect "toni and guy near me" searches unless each new listing is named and localised clearly.

355 pages, built for the search, not the brochure

Every page type on the preview site, sized by count. Each page answers one question a client types before choosing a salon. Measured from the site map.

"balayage vs highlights"
A comparison page gives the direct answer first, then a side-by-side table. 12 comparisons built.
"salon near hegde nagar"
A neighbourhood page says which salon is nearer and what the trip involves. 24 areas built.
"frizzy hair treatment bengaluru"
A concern guide starts from the problem, then the service. 20 concern guides built.

Answer-first pages are written so Google's AI answers and assistants can quote them (AEO), as well as rank them (SEO). Prices, phone numbers, hours and launch photos stay marked "To be confirmed" until the franchisee confirms them.

04

What a client sees today, and on opening day

Today

  • Searches "salon in Thanisandra": 36 salons on Maps, none of them this one.
  • Searches "TONI&GUY near me": other branches and Essensuals salons appear first.
  • No public website yet (preview only), no prices, no way to book.

Opening day (planned)

  • A verified listing per salon with services, prices, photos and booking.
  • 355 pages that answer the questions clients ask, each with a booking call to action.
  • Reviews asked for after every visit, and every one answered.
