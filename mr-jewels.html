The case in eight lines

Context
D2C jewellery brand. ₹9.21 Cr net revenue in the 12 months to July 2026, 20,850 orders, one showroom in Indiranagar. Meta took 78% of ad spend; I ran Google.
Problem
A 25-month-old Google account: ₹62.9 L spent, 30 campaigns, 80.9% on Shopping at ₹1,146 per conversion. Blended MER had fallen four quarters running to 2.68× in July.
Evidence
All-time and August Google exports, 13.7 lakh search terms, Merchant Center, Shopify sales, orders, sessions, returns and landing pages, billing history, the client's MER workbook.
Diagnosis
The conversion data was broken, so bidding was steering on fiction. The real losses were downstream: 85 of 100 carts never bought and 19.8% of gross sales came back.
Action
Fixed the showroom campaign's conversion goal, built brand search, three Performance Max asset groups and cart remarketing; specced the tracking repair, feed clean-up and call tracking. 24 documents.
Outcome
Showroom CPC −66%. August gross sales ₹1.07 Cr (+21.8%) on Google spend +143%. Incremental return 2.99× gross, 2.24× net.
Limitations
Cost of goods never supplied, so no campaign can be called profitable. August Meta spend missing, so no blended MER after July.
Next step
Attack returns, finish the tracking repair, move money from Shopping to what converts, clean the feed.

03

How I found it

Strip out every Google conversion number. Use only Shopify revenue and the billing screen. Then see which story survives.

The ₹12.56 Cr that never existed

One Shopping campaign booked ₹12.33 Cr between 1 and 4 August on ₹53,896 of spend. Strip those four days and the account converts about 13 times a day. Measured

July → August: what the extra money bought

Sessions up 53%, orders up 16%. The new traffic converted a quarter worse: buying wider, not better. Measured

Three real tracking defects

Purchase counted twice (Shopify app tag + GA4 import), inflating All conversions about 1.6×. An add-to-cart action carrying a value in the tens of billions of rupees. Goals with no primary action, so 35% of early-September "conversions" were showroom actions, not sales.

Read from conversion actions directly, 2 Sep · Measured

One false alarm, mine

"33 lifetime add-to-carts" had been read off goal-summary cards that count primary actions only. Add-to-cart actually fired 26,737 times. Purchase value was accurate: Google AOV ₹5,545 vs store ₹5,438.

Shopify could name the source of 3.4% of orders

96.5% of orders completed in a third-party checkout that emits no events. Shopify's own conversion rate read 0.01%; the true rate was 0.466%.

04

Diagnosis

The ad account wasn't the biggest leak. Bidding was blind, and the money that mattered was lost after the click.

85 / 100carts that never became orders. Cart→order fell from 16.8% to 13.5%. At 25% the store does ~99 orders a day on today's traffic. Calculated
₹2.41 Crreversed in 12 months, 19.8% of gross. One checkout carried 75.8% of sales at a 23.8% reversal rate, 3.6× the native store. Measured
5,562of 8,396 feed products never shown. 478 SKUs actually move. Ads pushed necklaces; the business runs on kadas. Measured

Not a marketing problem: returns and the checkout are operations and product decisions. Deity and animal pendants topped sell-through and came back at over 40%. ₹1,19,134 of August ad spend went to products with a 30%+ reversal history.

05

What I changed

Three campaigns launched, eleven asset and ad groups written, 24 documents shipped in 37 days.

The fix that paid: showroom campaign

Performance Max for the Indiranagar showroom read zero for 17 days. No local action was set to primary, so Smart Bidding bought anonymous clicks. One goal change on 21–25 Aug. Aug 1–17 vs Aug 1–25. Measured

Asset groups I wrote vs inherited

Cost per conversion, August. New groups convert on showroom actions (directions, calls), not sales; inherited ones mix both. Compare with care. Measured

Brand search, AG01–AG05

Exact, misspelling and competitor groups. Found the inherited "Brand Broad" buying non-brand traffic: ₹0.59 vs ₹7.42 CPC for the same brand on the same days.

Cart remarketing (C04)

Demand Gen on 0–7 day cart abandoners, three ads. A second group specced.

Negative list, built from 13.7 lakh terms

≈₹50,000 a month of defensible cuts. Kept competitor terms that converted at ₹87.

Specs handed over

Tracking repair, feed rules (one rule releases ~5,656 products), call tracking via a split Exotel build, non-brand Search, Stage 5 Performance Max on the 478 movers.

Built: 3 campaigns · Not built before close: 6 specs

07

What I got wrong

A case study that only lists wins is a sales page. Seven calls, owned. The four that cost the most:

Raised the brand budget against my own spec

Hypothesis

More brand budget captures more branded demand.

Action

₹2,000 → ₹3,100 a day on 17 Aug, while my spec the same day said cap at ₹150–200. Measured brand demand was ~₹64 a day.

Result

₹57,249 at ₹2,408 per conversion vs ₹36 on the inherited brand campaign. Surplus went into broad match.

Learning

Caught on 24 Aug. Budget follows measured demand, not ambition.

Let spend scale on an unverified value signal

Hypothesis

Campaigns reporting high value deserve more budget.

Action

CatchAll scaled 537%, Stand_14Jan 50%.

Result

Both were reporting impossible value. Google-only MER fell 19.61× → 9.82×.

Learning

Reconcile Google purchases to Shopify weekly. Stop if Google claims more than 60% of orders.

Carried a false alarm through four documents

Hypothesis

Purchase tracking is broken: only 33 add-to-carts ever.

Action

Gated scaling and planned a pixel rebuild on it.

Result

A week of gated decisions. It was a goal-card reading error.

Learning

Read the source, not the summary card.

Remarketing without a stop rule

Hypothesis

Cart abandoners convert on a reminder.

Action

C04 live with three ads.

Result

₹11,971, 3,118 clicks, zero purchases by 2 Sep.

Learning

Set the stop-or-continue date before launch.

Also owned

Linear-scaled a partial month (July MER published as 3.20×, restated to 2.68× the same day). Misread a 100-row sample as a broken export. Dated Dhanteras ten days early. Every correction was written into the document it corrected.

08

What I'd do next

42 issues on the register at close. 16 were never marketing problems.

1 · Attack the reversal rate

Monthly returns report, an RTO vs return split, prepaid incentives on the 40%+ pendant lines. Ask why one checkout reverses at 3.6× the native one.

Owner: client ops · ~₹5 L a month at stake · Estimate

2 · Finish the tracking repair

One purchase action, add-to-cart with no value, showroom actions secondary, enhanced conversions on, a 7-day Google-vs-Shopify reconciliation. Then bid on value net of reversals.

3 · Fix the cart before buying more traffic

Three routes to 100 orders a day: buy it (₹45–52 L a month, breaks the 3.50× floor), fix cart completion (₹0 media), or non-brand Search (+4–7 a day). The cart is the cheapest.

Estimate

4 · Get the two numbers that decide everything

Gross margin by category and monthly Meta spend. Without them nobody can say whether the account is profitable. And move billing to auto-pay: it went dark twice in August.
