05 · Under the hood

Built to be trusted with real data

  • Private by default. Owner login only. Guest and patient contact details visible to owners and editors, nobody else.
  • No keys in the browser. Ad, CRM and call-tracking credentials stay server-side.
  • Every number labelled. Measured, calculated or estimated. Where data is missing, the page says so.
  • Runs itself. Scheduled syncs, from every 5 minutes to daily. No spreadsheets to update.
