An audit of my own numbers

Kept in on purpose. A case that only lists wins is a sales page.

My own ROAS headline

TriedPublished "34× lifetime ROAS, $787.9K revenue".
GotIt was 52,560 leads × $15. Not money received.
LearnedIf it can't be traced to the bank, it doesn't headline.

Conversion rates above 100%

TriedReported peak-month CVR of 214.9%.
GotSeveral actions fired per visitor. Counts inflated.
LearnedOne primary conversion per meaningful action.

Budget 3× in June 2025

Tried$1,520 → $5,140 a month.
GotLeads didn't follow. Cut to $1,140 by August.
LearnedCut fast when quality erodes. That part worked.
