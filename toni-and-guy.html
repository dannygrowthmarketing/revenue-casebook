Level 1

The 60-second story

For the CEO. Read left to right.
  1. 1Problem

    Sales growth had stalled at +1.3% on last year in Jan-Feb 2026 because average bill fell 13.5%, while Google ran an inherited, unstructured account.

  2. 2The leak

    Leads rose but each returned less: revenue per Google lead fell from ₹3,116 to ₹1,361, and ad money drifted to low-return salons.

  3. 3How I found it

    Joined 20 months of till data to Google exports, then in August joined ad spend to point-of-sale revenue salon by salon.

  4. 4What I did

    Rebuilt Google per salon, launched per-salon Meta, moved the site to Netlify, built a booking app, reported monthly with salon P&L.

  5. 5What changed

    Mar-Aug 2026 revenue +14.7% (₹14.04 to 16.10 Cr), bills +21.6%, on Google spend +74%. Part is seasonal or new-salon capacity.

  6. 6Proof

    20-month point-of-sale and Google Ads ledger (Jan 2025 to Aug 2026) plus the August salon P&L, from the monthly reports.

  7. 7Lesson

    Spend, clicks and leads rose faster than bills. Judge ad money by bills and salon-level return, and test lead counts for definition changes.

What the history shows

Patterns found by re-reading the full history, not just the months I ran. Each is labelled: measured pattern, correlation, anomaly or seasonality.

Seasonality

Feb to Aug is a seasonal climb: 2025 did +49.5% with no rebuild; Feb 2026 was not the worst month

Revenue rises Feb to Aug every year. 2025 climbed ₹1.84 to 2.75 Cr; 2026 ₹1.91 to 2.94 Cr. Bills climbed less in 2026 than in 2025. Feb 2025 (₹1.84 Cr) is below Feb 2026, so the 'worst month in the record' line contradicts the ledger. Feb is 28 days: ₹6.82 L a day, above Jan 2026 (₹6.65 L) and Feb 2025 (₹6.57 L).

The numbers

Rev Feb to Aug: 2025 +49.5%, 2026 +53.9% (reports print +54.1%). Bills: 2025 +33.8%, 2026 +29.4%. Seasonal-shape Aug 2026 = ₹2.85 Cr vs actual ₹2.94 Cr (+3.0%). Per day, Feb 2026 ranks 4th lowest of 20 months.

So whatDo not use Feb-to-Aug as the result; use YoY. Feb 2026 is not the low point of the 20 months; Feb 2025 is. One year of seasonality only: no 2024 data.

Measured pattern

About 70% of the growth acceleration is average bill recovering, not more bills

YoY revenue went from +1.3% (Jan-Feb) to +14.7% (Mar-Aug). Bills growth moved +17.1% to +21.6%; average-bill gap moved -13.5% to -5.7%. Log decomposition gives about 30% to bills and 70% to ticket size. The fall started Dec 2025 (₹2,774), reached ₹2,439 in Jan and ₹2,359 in Feb, before the engagement.

The numbers

₹2,942 (Oct 2025) to ₹2,359 (Feb 2026) = -19.8%; Dec to Jan -12.1%. Mar-Aug 2026 weighted ₹2,682 vs ₹2,844 (-5.7%). Contribution: bills 30.2%, average bill 69.8% (calculated).

So whatThe +1.3% pre-join trend is depressed by an average-bill trough, so the 'above trend' credit to media in any above-trend attribution is probably overstated. Volume growth (+17%) was already running before March.

Anomaly

The lead step-change and the dilution happened in Oct 2025, four months before I joined

MQLs jumped 10,399 to 17,665 in Oct 2025 with CTR 1.28% to 2.40% (3.58% by Nov) and impressions halved by Nov. Revenue per MQL fell from ₹3,116 (Jan-Sep 2025) to ₹1,473 (Oct-Dec 2025), then ₹1,361 in Mar-Aug 2026. Oct-Dec 2025 averaged 17,607 MQLs a month; Mar-Aug 2026 averaged 19,709 (+11.9%). Jan-Feb 2026 test: bills +17.0% YoY in Jan with MQLs -20% YoY and spend -43%; +17.2% in Feb with MQLs +202%.

The numbers

Bills per 1,000 MQLs 1,106 to 512 to 508. Avg monthly MQLs: 7,192 (Jan-Sep 2025), 17,607 (Oct-Dec), 19,709 (Mar-Aug 2026). 58% of the CPC fall Jan 2025 to Aug 2026 (₹14.23 to 4.58) was done by Feb 2026.

So whatPart of the '+165% leads' is a step that predates the rebuild, possibly a conversion-action change (not stated). Month-to-month MQL volume has not tracked bills in the one clean test. Audit MQL definition before quoting lead growth as demand.

Correlation, not causation

Aug 2026 repeated the Oct 2025 spend spike: revenue per Google rupee 38-39x both times

Oct 2025: spend +130% on Sep, revenue +10.0%, bills +2.4%, 38x. Dec 2025 then bought 95% of Oct's MQLs on 44% of the spend, with higher revenue (111%). Aug 2026: Google spend +75% on Jul, revenue +9.7%, bills +3.5%, 39x. In 2025 Jul to Aug on flat spend revenue rose +16.5%, bills +10.2%. May 2026 record per day (₹9.58 L) came on ₹3.43 L spend; Aug per day ₹9.48 L on ₹7.60 L.

The numbers

Seasonal-shape Aug 2026 from Jul = ₹3.12 Cr vs actual ₹2.94 Cr (-5.9%). Rev/day May to Aug 2026 -1.0% while Google spend +121.6% (2025 same span +15.5% per day, spend +14.3%). YoY revenue: Apr-May +25.7% on spend +50.9%; Jun-Aug +10.1% on spend +101.2%. Marginal Oct 2025: ₹50 per extra MQL, ₹6 revenue per extra rupee.

So whatAt about ₹6.4 L+ a month Google spend has twice bought leads, not revenue. Peak efficiency months (Apr-May, ₹3.4-3.5 L) were the strongest. Season, new salons and Meta also moved, so causation is unproven.

Measured pattern

August spend is inversely related to salon revenue: 3 salons took 15% of ad money for 43% of revenue, 4 took 53% for 13%

Phoenix Mall of Asia, Phoenix MarketCity and Lulu billed 42.7% of Aug revenue on 15.0% of salon-attributed ad spend. Sadashivanagar, Nexus Vega City, Koramangala 4th Block and Hennur billed 12.6% on 53.3%. Correlation of salon revenue with ad spend is negative. The three low-return new-ish salons have average bill ₹2,278 vs ₹2,876 elsewhere.

The numbers

Salon-attributed ad spend ₹6,52,433 (of ₹10,32,325 total). Top 5 salons 57.8% of revenue, 22.6% of spend. r(revenue, spend) = -0.27 across 15 salons. Koramangala 4th, Vega City, Hennur: 8.5% of revenue, 43.9% of spend, 8.7x vs 71.5x for the rest. Top 3 salons also ₹3,268 average bill. HHI 936 (about 10.7 equal-size salons).

So whatSpend followed new-store push and targets, not return. One month only: no earlier salon-level revenue, so the trend is unknown. Network average bill gap (₹63 of ₹170) may partly be mix from low-ticket salons; Aug 2025 mix not available.

Not a marketing problem

What the data shows ads cannot fix. Saying so early saves budget.

  • Average bill ₹2,813 vs ₹2,983 in Aug 2025 (-5.7%); ₹17.78 L a month at Aug volume. Product 5.4% and packages 28.1% of sales. Menu, package and stylist conversation. It began falling in Dec 2025.
  • Follow-up: 1,112 of 1,272 Meta leads uncalled in June (12.6% called), 18 of 50 outcomes wrong numbers. Salon calling discipline, not ad delivery.
  • Mantri Avenue 81% of target (₹1,74,114 gap) with zero ads; funding is a client budget decision.
  • New salons bill below network average ticket: Vega City ₹2,166 (90% of target), K 4th Block ₹2,214, Hennur ₹2,685 (85% of target).
  • POS gaps: Koramangala 8th Block (₹38,518 spend, 2,173 leads) and Lavelle Road (₹4,561) have no POS row in Aug.
  • Seasonality: Sep 2025 vs Aug 2025 revenue -19.6%, bills -12.4%, average bill -8.4%. September is structurally softer.

Data not available

What would prove more, and does not exist in the sources yet.

  • Any data before Jan 2025: seasonality rests on one year (2025), so trend and season cannot be separated.
  • Who ran the account before 28 Feb 2026 and why spend spiked Oct 2025 (₹6.43 L) and was cut Jan 2026 (₹1.19 L); what changed in Oct 2025 (CTR 1.28% to 3.58%, impressions -48% by Nov).
  • Conversion-action definition: MQLs are calls, directions, store visits and web combined, with no split over time; only a June GBP action mix exists. A definition change in Oct 2025 cannot be ruled out.
  • Aug 6-12 conversion spike unresolved: Aug MQLs (22,532) and CPA ₹33.71 may be overstated, and the six-month totals include them.
  • Lead-to-booking data: CRM call log only for June; GBP actions not connected; no app bookings; no booking-by-source.
  • Salon-level revenue before Aug 2026, opening dates of Nexus Vega City, Lavelle Road, Koramangala 4th and 8th Block, so new-salon share of growth cannot be measured (any split is an assumption).
  • Salon count: reports say 16, but 18 names appear (POS has Mantri Avenue; K 8th Block and Lavelle Road have spend, no POS row). Whether the ₹2.94 Cr includes all salons is unresolved.
  • Margin by service, discounting or price changes, new vs repeat clients, retention, review ratings over time.
  • Meta before May 2026 and a like-for-like Meta result series (Aug 'results' mixes objectives; Jun/Jul are leads).
  • Dates of the site migration, domain switch and tag fix; whether the tag fires on toniandguyhairdressing.com.
  • Who created the 30 Jul Demand Gen ('by Ega') and who raised Demand Gen and K 4th PMAX spend in Aug after the July issue advised otherwise.
  • Sep 2026 actuals (modelled only: hold ₹2.53 Cr). The model has bills -6.1% on Aug, against -12.4% in 2025; the 2025 shape alone would give about ₹2.36 Cr.
  • Casebook items not in these four reports: payment gateway, iOS app, WhatsApp follow-up 'rolling out', 16 Business Profiles clean-up. Final report calls the WhatsApp loop 'proposed, not built'.
  • Several Jan-Jun 2026 figures are rounded in the source (e.g. 22,90,000 impressions, 1,30,000 clicks, 18,700 MQLs); May 2026 and Jun 2026 values were restated between issues.

Tests and what they taught

Not every test worked. Each one changed the next decision.

  1. Hypothesis

    Demand Gen adds network-level demand at near-account cost (hypothesis not written in sources; implied by launch)

  2. Action

    Two Demand Gen campaigns, 30 Jul and Aug 2026

  3. Result

    July ₹4,001, 0 conversions. Aug ₹1,36,752 for 173 conversions (₹790 each) vs ₹33.71 account.

  4. Learning

    No version converted near account rates; the July issue had already advised killing it and August spend rose about 34x.

  5. Next decision

    Switch off, move budget to store PMax and Search (Issue Nº08, 3 Sep). Execution after exit not in sources.

  1. Hypothesis

    Scaling Koramangala 4th Block PMAX lifts that salon's billing

  2. Action

    PMAX spend ₹67,466 (Jul) to ₹1,27,776 (Aug, +89%)

  3. Result

    1,609 conversions at ₹79 (Jul ₹60.66). Salon billed ₹7.46 L: 4.9x on ₹1,53,007 all-in, ₹454 media per bill.

  4. Learning

    Salon beat a small target (112%) at the worst return in the network; July advice was rebuild assets before spending more.

  5. Next decision

    Cap at ₹60,000, rebuild asset group.

  1. Hypothesis

    Moving Meta budget from the seven CUT salons to the seven SCALE salons lowers lead cost (July proposal)

  2. Action

    Proposed in Issue Nº07; Aug campaign lines for the CUT salons still show about ₹6.2 K each

  3. Result

    Aug cost per result ₹31.74 vs about ₹50, but results mix lead forms, WhatsApp chats and calls; reallocation not visible in campaign spend.

  4. Learning

    The ₹31.74 is driven by K 8th Block WhatsApp at ₹7.78 and Whitefield at ₹11.76, not by the proposed reallocation.

  5. Next decision

    Compare like for like by objective; reduce duplicate campaigns (9 salons run two).

  1. Hypothesis

    Click-to-WhatsApp buys cheaper results than lead forms for the same salon (observed, not a controlled test)

  2. Action

    Parallel campaigns per salon in Aug

  3. Result

    WhatsApp cheaper in 5 of 7 salons with both (K 8th ₹8 vs 21, Whitefield 12 vs 24, Sadashivanagar 25 vs 40, K 4th 45 vs 72, MarketCity 44 vs 47); equal at Vega City; dearer at Phoenix Mall of Asia (149 vs 84).

  4. Learning

    Results are different commitments, so unit cost is not comparable.

  5. Next decision

    Tie each objective to calls and bills via CRM log before shifting budget.
